About me

From navigation radar to backend

I'm a backend developer working mainly with Go (Fiber) and Python (FastAPI) on PostgreSQL, MongoDB and Redis, with Docker and CI/CD on GitHub Actions. I've built five systems of my own applying clean architecture, asynchronous queues with retries, JWT/RBAC authentication and automated testing.

My current focus is production APIs and language-model integration: RAG, MCP servers and orchestration across multiple LLM providers.

I spent 14 years with the Argentine Navy maintaining and repairing navigation radar systems, finishing as Electronic Repairs Supervisor leading a technical team. From there I bring systematic troubleshooting under pressure, on-call discipline, rigorous documentation and work with a minimal margin of error. What interested me most about those systems was the logic behind how they process information, and in 2022 I turned to software.
